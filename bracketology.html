Method and limits

  • RPI rebuilt from game results with the NCAA formula and non-conference bonus/penalty table; matches the NCAA adjusted values (correlation 0.998).
  • Team ratings from an ordered-logit model anchored on last year's rank; remaining games simulated 3,000 times.
  • At-large model: logistic regression on RPI rank, SoS rank, non-conference opponent strength, conference strength, and two gaps — Massey rank vs RPI rank and Top-50 results rank vs RPI rank (added Oct 12, 2026) — trained on every committee field 2007–2025. The gaps capture teams whose results are better or worse than their RPI says; they raised the leave-one-year-out score from 90.8% to 91.8% and cut probability log loss by about 16%.
  • Massey and Top-50 results ranks are rebuilt from game results and simulated with the RPI (they match the published sheets at 0.98 and 0.93–0.95 rank correlation, so live values are close but not identical).
  • Weekly trend: each weekly run is saved as a snapshot. Arrows compare with the snapshot at least six days earlier. Earlier weeks (from Sep 6) were re-simulated from the results available at those dates, so they use the same model but with the information of that date.
  • Conference tournaments are not simulated; neutral sites are untagged; a few teams have small game-count gaps vs the NCAA table; selection day assumed Nov 9, 2026.
