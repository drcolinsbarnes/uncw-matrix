Method and limits

  • RPI rebuilt from game results with the NCAA formula and non-conference bonus/penalty table; matches the NCAA adjusted values (correlation 0.998).
  • Team ratings from an ordered-logit model anchored on last year's rank; remaining games simulated 3,000 times.
  • At-large model: logistic regression on RPI rank, SoS rank, non-conference opponent strength and conference strength, trained on every committee field 2007–2025.
  • Conference tournaments are not simulated; neutral sites are untagged; a few teams have small game-count gaps vs the NCAA table; selection day assumed Nov 9, 2026.
